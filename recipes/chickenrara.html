Chicken Rara

Description

  • 500g chicken pieces
  • 200g chicken mince
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan and add the chopped onions. Cook until golden brown.
  2. Add minced garlic and ginger paste. Sauté for a minute.
  3. Add chicken mince and cook until it changes color.
  4. Add all the spices (turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala) and salt. Mix well.
  5. Add chicken pieces and cook for 10-15 minutes on low heat until tender.
  6. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves before serving.

    7. Chicken Rara is a delicious and hearty dish that combines tender chicken pieces with a flavorful gravy made from chicken mince.

    The dish is typically seasoned with a blend of spices, creating a rich and savory flavor profile. It is often served with rice or bread, making it a satisfying meal for any occasion.
