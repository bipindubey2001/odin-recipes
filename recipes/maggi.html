Maggi with Egg

Description

  • 1 1⁄2 cups (350 ml) of water
  • 1 2.46-ounce (70 g) packets of Maggi noodles
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup (50 g) of grated cheese
  • Salt and ground pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil water in a saucepan. Add the Maggi noodles and cook for 2 minutes.
  2. Crack the egg into the saucepan and stir it into the noodles. Cook for another 2 minutes until the egg is fully cooked.
  3. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Sprinkle grated cheese on top and let it melt before serving.

Maggi is a popular instant noodle brand known for its quick and easy preparation. It is often used in various recipes and can be enjoyed as a simple snack or meal.

The dish is typically seasoned with a blend of spices, creating a rich and savory flavor profile. It is often served with rice or bread, making it a satisfying meal for any occasion.
